Featured · Latest · The Journal of cell biology · 6 Nov 2026

Developmental elimination of electrical synapses by UNC-51/UNC-76-mediated vesicular transport.

Review
Journal provisional
Subjects
Animals
Notices
No notices

✓ Verified summary

Researchers studying sensory neurons in the roundworm C. elegans found that electrical synapses, which are direct physical connections between cells, are temporarily present in early life before being removed as the nervous system matures.

These early electrical connections cause rapid fluctuations in calcium levels, a signaling molecule within the cells, and using light to destroy them stops these fluctuations while preventing the formation of standard chemical synapses.

However, restoring the calcium activity without the electrical connections fixes the issue with chemical synapse formation, indicating that the calcium signal itself is crucial for proper development.
