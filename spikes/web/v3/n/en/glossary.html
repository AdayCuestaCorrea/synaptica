    Gaps and proposals in this mock-up

    What is still missing or undecided. On the page it carries a tag that links here.

    • Gap Type of each item: set by the classifier (step 14a), not run yet. Only «Clinical trial» already comes from PubMed's publication type.
    • Gap Grouping: «the same story in several outlets» is decided by the LLM (step 15), not run yet. Each item appears alone, in the same component that will show the group.
    • Gap The verified summaries are those from S5 (studies of 2 to 8 November); the new chain has not run yet.
    • Gap The verified weekly summary does not exist yet (it needs the LLM): its fallback text, built from the data, is shown.
    • Proposal News images: figure 1 (or the graphical abstract) of PMC open-access articles with a CC licence, resized and never cropped. Many are charts: whether to pick another one, or let Sofía pick, is undecided. Nature, Science, Cell and the media have no image.
    • Proposal Proposal for step 26: a number computed from the warehouse, no LLM.
    • Gap Provisional wording: «published in a journal» does not prove peer review. Sofía decides what this signal is called.
    • Gap Includes noise not filtered yet (step 14a).
    • Proposal Provisional topics (to be revisited when there is more news). Each topic is a branch of the MeSH vocabulary of the U.S. National Library of Medicine. A study indexed in PubMed or Europe PMC takes the topics of its MeSH; the rest (recent records and every preprint), the MeSH synonyms in its title or keywords, or at least twice in its abstract. Measured against MeSH on 178 studies: the text rule is right between 60 % (Emotion) and 100 % (Memory, Sleep) of the time, with few cases per topic. Media news only have a title. «Emotion» brushes against what v13 left out: Sofía confirms.

    • Gap Draft: Aday and Sofía write their own introduction. Only what SCOPE and the proposal already say is here.
    • Proposal Search: over what the site has today (studies, news, sections, topics and acronyms), in the browser, with no outside service. Adapted from Paleo Atlas.
    • Proposal Illustrations: our own SVG drawings (CC BY-SA 4.0), in the style Sofía chose with a bit more detail; also the brain of Neuro Atlas, which replaces the viewer captures.
