    Emotion

    Today's studies and news about emotion. ProposalProposal: Provisional topics (to be revisited when there is more news). Each topic is a branch of the MeSH vocabulary of the U.S. National Library of Medicine. A study indexed in PubMed or Europe PMC takes the topics of its MeSH; the rest (recent records and every preprint), the MeSH synonyms in its title or keywords, or at least twice in its abstract. Measured against MeSH on 178 studies: the text rule is right between 60 % (Emotion) and 100 % (Memory, Sleep) of the time, with few cases per topic. Media news only have a title. «Emotion» brushes against what v13 left out: Sofía confirms.
